# **App Name**: Zenith Anime

## Core Features:

- Global Anime Search: Browse and search through thousands of anime titles powered by comprehensive metadata providers.
- Personalized Watchlist: Organize titles into 'Plan to Watch', 'Watching', and 'Completed' categories with a seamless management interface.
- Intelligent Discovery Tool: A generative AI tool that analyzes your viewing history to reason and recommend specific titles that match your unique tastes.
- Real-time Episode Tracking: Quickly update your progress and set alerts for new episodes of currently airing series.
- Data Synchronization: Ensure your watchlist and preferences are persisted and synced across devices via a cloud storage solution.
- Genre Visualizer: A sleek dashboard providing visual insights into your watching habits and favorite genres.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft Lavender (#A89BFF), reflecting the magical and artistic essence of anime while remaining easy on the eyes.
- Background color: Deep Midnight (#161321), providing a dark, high-contrast canvas that lets vibrant anime posters stand out.
- Accent color: Celestial Blue (#5E89F0), used for action buttons and highlighting critical navigation paths.
- Headline font: 'Poppins' - a geometric sans-serif that lends a contemporary and fashionable feel to the application's core headings.
- Body font: 'Inter' - a modern, objective sans-serif used for descriptions and technical details to ensure high legibility.
- Minimalist, thin-stroke line icons that evoke a clean, modern aesthetic without cluttering the rich visual media.
- Subtle card-scaling transitions and soft blurs for page shifts, creating a premium, boutique agency-level browsing experience.
